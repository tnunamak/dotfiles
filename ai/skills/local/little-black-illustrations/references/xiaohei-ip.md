# Little Black (小黑) IP

## Character definition

Little Black (小黑) is the fixed visual IP for these Chinese article body illustrations.

By default Little Black appears in every image. Little Black is not a mascot, not a
sticker, not cute decoration — it is an absurd worker earnestly taking part in running
the system.

## Appearance

- A small solid-black creature.
- White round dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole mouth,
  or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline, with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, dazed, calm, earnest.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, doesn't ham it up for cuteness.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Like it genuinely owns some job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Give Little Black the core action:

- Hauling material.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a break point.
- Working a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Slicing the "material fish".
- Stamping the hand-off script.
- Leading the hand-off path.
- Holding a warning sign while watching a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Beside the scene: hauling bricks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting,
  recording.

## Forbidden

- Don't draw Little Black as an over-cute mascot.
- Don't make it a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Little Black complex clothing, meme expressions, or sparkly eyes.
- Don't let Little Black just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Little Black steal the structural message.
- Don't draw Little Black too commercial, too round, too polished.

## Test

If you remove Little Black and the image's core metaphor still fully holds, then Little
Black was too decorative; rewrite the prompt so Little Black becomes the action subject.
