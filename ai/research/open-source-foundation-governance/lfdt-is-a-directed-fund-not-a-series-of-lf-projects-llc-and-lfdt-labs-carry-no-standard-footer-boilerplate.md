## CLAIMS

- "A Series of LF Projects, LLC" is a specific Delaware series-LLC legal wrapper the Linux Foundation uses for *some* hosted projects, each project becoming its own liability-segregated series of one master LLC; it is governed by policies published at lfprojects.org. [lf-projects-trademark-policy]
- LF Decentralized Trust (LFDT) is chartered as a **"Directed Fund" of The Linux Foundation**, a categorically different legal vehicle, per LFDT's own published charter. [lfdt-charter]
- LFDT's own website footer copyright line is "Copyright © The Linux Foundation®. All rights reserved. LF Decentralized Trust is a trademark of The Linux Foundation." — it does not use "Series of LF Projects, LLC" language. [lfdt-footer]
- LFDT publishes a suggested trademark-notice template for projects to adapt: "Copyright Acme 2021. All rights reserved. LF Decentralized Trust is a trademark of The Linux Foundation. See lfdecentralizedtrust.org" (with "Acme" as a literal fill-in-the-name placeholder, not a real entity). [lfdt-trademark-guidelines]
- This LFDT-suggested template makes no distinction for Labs (LFDT's early-stage incubation tier) vs. graduated/hosted projects, and no LFDT document found states whether Labs are required, expected, or not expected to carry any copyright/trademark footer line at all. [lfdt-trademark-guidelines]
- x402 — which does carry "Copyright (c) x402 a Series of LF Projects, LLC..." on x402.org — is its own independently-chartered "x402 Foundation," organized both as a Directed Fund with its own Governing Board *and* separately as its own distinct Series of LF Projects, LLC, per its official Participation Agreement. [x402-participation-agreement]
- The x402 Foundation has premier paying members (Coinbase, Circle, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, AWS, Stripe) funding a dedicated governance/funding vehicle, per Linux Foundation press materials — a materially heavier and differently-chartered structure than an LFDT Labs-tier incubation project, making it a poor comparable for lab-tier footer boilerplate. [x402-lf-press]
- GitMesh, a currently-listed LFDT Lab, uses an informal, non-templated footer line — "GitMesh CE is governed by Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust. This website is hosted by Alveoli for community welfare." — with no copyright line and no Series-LLC language, and does not follow LFDT's own suggested trademark-notice template either. [gitmesh-footer]
- The LFDT Labs proposal process document carries forward Hyperledger-era branding language: Labs may not publicly call themselves a "project" of the foundation; the sanctioned phrasing is "a [Foundation] Lab" (e.g. historically "a Hyperledger Lab"). This document sets no footer-copyright requirement and never mentions "Series of LF Projects, LLC." [lfdt-labs-proposal-process]
- The LFDT Labs index (github.com/LF-Decentralized-Trust-labs and lf-decentralized-trust-labs.github.io/labs/) is the canonical, up-to-date list of accepted labs — a project's absence as a repo/listing there is evidence it has not (yet) been accepted, and its presence is evidence of accepted status. [lfdt-labs-index]

## SYNTHESIS

The "Copyright (c) X a Series of LF Projects, LLC" line seen on some Linux Foundation
project sites (e.g. x402.org) is not generic LF boilerplate — it names a specific,
opt-in Delaware legal structure ("a Series of LF Projects, LLC") that only some LF
projects actually use. LF Decentralized Trust (LFDT) itself is chartered under a
different vehicle (a Directed Fund of The Linux Foundation), and its own site reflects
that with a plain "Copyright © The Linux Foundation" line, not Series-LLC language. This
means any project or lab living inside LFDT should not copy the x402-style line by
default — doing so would misstate the entity's actual legal structure.

For LFDT Labs specifically (the early-stage incubation tier, one step below a graduated
project), there is no confirmed mandatory footer boilerplate. LFDT does publish a
suggested trademark-notice template ("Copyright [Name] [Year]... trademark of The Linux
Foundation... See lfdecentralizedtrust.org"), but no LFDT document states this applies to
Labs specifically, and the one real Lab footer checked (GitMesh) doesn't use it — Labs
appear to operate with informal, inconsistent, or absent copyright/trademark lines in
practice, consistent with their AS-IS, pre-formal-governance status.

Practical implication for future LFDT-lab website work: (1) never default to "a Series
of LF Projects, LLC" without confirming the specific entity actually uses that structure
— check the entity's own charter or participation agreement, not just its parent
foundation's name; (2) for LFDT Labs, the safe default is minimal/no legal boilerplate
beyond an accurate governance statement (e.g. "X is an LF Decentralized Trust Lab"),
since that's both accurate and matches observed real-world lab practice; (3) if a lab
wants to add copyright/trademark language, LFDT's own suggested template is the best
available anchor, but confirming with LFDT staff (trademark@lfdecentralizedtrust.org)
before publishing is warranted given the lack of a documented labs-tier requirement.
