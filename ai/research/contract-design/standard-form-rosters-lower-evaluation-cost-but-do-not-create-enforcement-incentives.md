## CLAIMS

- Creative Commons provides legal-code, human-readable, and machine-readable layers over a roster of six standard licenses; it reported 2.5 billion licensed works in 2022. [cc-layers] [cc-scale]
- A tracking study of 48,154 retail software shoppers across 90 firms found only one or two per thousand accessed the license. [fine-print]
- Radin describes modern boilerplate as combining modularity with waning consent and argues that repeated standard forms can displace meaningful agreement. [radin]
- IEEE 7012 uses standard forms kept by an independent neutral roster and requires matching signed records, but its located pilot exposed substantial legal and implementation review cost on the recipient side. [ieee]
- GPC has a narrow, legislatively recognized consequence backed by public enforcement. RSL and TDMRep publish richer machine-readable terms, but no primary evidence located in this research showed major crawlers accepting or complying with them. [gpc] [rsl]

## SYNTHESIS

Rosters solve recognition and evaluation cost, not bargaining power. Creative Commons works because a small set of stable terms overlays a clear copyright exclusion right and widespread reuse demand. In personal-data exchange, the service usually controls access to the product and gains little from reviewing user-authored obligations. A personal agent reduces the user's evaluation cost but cannot remove the recipient's legal and implementation cost. Adoption therefore still needs law, platform policy, market leverage, certification, or access that the recipient values enough to accept conditions.
